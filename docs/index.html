A bull agent and a bear agent argue from the same evidence. A published, hash-registered policy decides. Each decision goes on-chain with its evidence hash and policy hash, so anyone can replay it in this page and dispute it on-chain.

Why this is "duty of care" and not just a bot

		

			
Reasonable care, provable later. The evidence the agent relied on is hashed and committed when the decision is made. Nobody can quietly swap it afterwards.

			
Known rules, fixed in advance. The policy is hash-registered on-chain before it can be used. A report under any other rulebook is rejected by the contract.

			
Contestable. Anyone who replays a decision and gets a different answer can call dispute() to leave a permanent on-chain record.
